Skyscraper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Skyscraper ("the game") shows ads from Google AdMob. The developer does not collect or store any personal data; the ads service below may collect some device data as described here.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (best height, Tips, unlocked climbers and outfits, settings and language) in a file on your device only. This file never leaves your device through the game.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this save file in your device backup so your progress can move to a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your own account and settings; the developer cannot see it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for a revive, double Tips or a free box) and a full-screen ad after some lost runs. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location from the IP, and ad interaction data, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first (Google's consent form). You can reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
  • No location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts or files are accessed.

Permissions: Internet and Advertising ID (for the ads) and Vibrate (haptic feedback).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes the save file.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
